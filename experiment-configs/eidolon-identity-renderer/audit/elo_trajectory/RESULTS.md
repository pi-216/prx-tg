| i20 | 2 / 25 | [4, 7, 8, 9, 10] | [1, 2, 3, 5, 6] | overlap |

**4 of 6 pools separate perfectly.** Under the null (random ordering) a fixed 5-item set lands in the bottom 5 with p = 1/252 per pool, so ≥4/6 perfect gives **p ≈ 3.7e-09**.

## Exploratory: does detection attrition track the human ranking?

Attrition = renders of that checkpoint in which Sapiens2 seg2 finds no usable
skin mask (no face+neck). Lower rank = better render, so a USEFUL proxy needs a
POSITIVE correlation with mean rank (more attrition -> worse).
